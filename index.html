Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery usually take?

Most orders are delivered within 30-45 minutes. However, during peak hours or extreme weather conditions, it might take a bit longer. You can always track your order in real-time!

Is there a minimum order amount for delivery?

Yes, the minimum order amount for delivery is $10.00. This helps us keep our delivery services fast and efficient for everyone.

How can I earn and redeem CravePoints?

You earn 2 points for every $1 spent! Once you hit 100 points, you can unlock special rewards. To redeem them, just click on the 'Gift' icon near your cart when you have items added!

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Orders can be cancelled within 2 minutes of placement. After that, our kitchen usually starts preparing your meal. Please contact our support team immediately if you need assistance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and Cash on Delivery. You can select your preferred method at the checkout page.
